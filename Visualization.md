# The real opportunity: a "visualization engine"

I wouldn't build this as merely:

**“An app containing thousands of pictures.”**

I'd build:

> **A library of interactive learning models mapped to curriculum indicators.**

Each visualization would have metadata such as:

```text
Subject: Integrated Science
Level: Basic 6
Strand: Diversity of Matter
Topic: Photosynthesis
Indicator: ...
Concept: Factors affecting photosynthesis

Visualization:
    photosynthesis-rate-model

Inputs:
    Light
    Carbon dioxide
    Water

Outputs:
    Photosynthesis rate
    Glucose
    Oxygen

Teacher mode:
    Explain
    Demonstrate
    Question
    Assess

Learner mode:
    Explore
    Predict
    Manipulate
    Discover
```

That changes the product completely.

The teacher isn't searching for **“a photosynthesis picture.”**

They're searching for:

**Basic 6 → Photosynthesis → Explore factors affecting photosynthesis**

and the appropriate visualization appears.
