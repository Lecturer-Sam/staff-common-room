### The problem

Your teachers spend Sunday nights handwriting lesson plans and schemes of learning.
Coverage varies classroom to classroom. And when GES or a parent asks *"what exactly has
this class been taught?"* — nobody can answer with evidence.

### What Beacon gives you

**For your teachers**
- **180 fully-written daily lesson plans** per subject, per year — 36 weeks, 3 terms
- **73 volumes** covering Basic 1–9 and 13 subjects — **13,140 lessons** in total
- Every plan in the national GES/NaCCA format: Starter (5 min) → Main (20 min) →
  Plenary (5 min), with resources, assessment and a reflection block
- Every plan tagged to a **NaCCA indicator code** (`B4.1.1.1.1`)
- Termly **Schemes of Learning** generated in the approved layout
- Delivered as editable Word, so teachers adapt rather than copy

**For you**
- A **live coverage dashboard**: see class by class, indicator by indicator, what has been
  taught — and what has been skipped
- **Consistent teaching quality** across every classroom, including new and untrained staff
- **Evidence for inspection**, ready to print
- Onboarding and teacher training included
